Costs are explicit in every figure: 85% round-trip efficiency and 0.50 $/MWh fees on every MWh moved — net is never reported as gross. The oracle is the best possible 2×2 hour choice with hindsight, same battery, same costs. Capture = P&L ÷ oracle P&L; tau = Kendall tau-b of the committed forecast vs the actual day.

Paper money — no capital at stake. Every receipt is committed and pushed before the DAM publishes (~13:30 CT); the ledger is append-only and OpenTimestamps-stamped — each day's proof is submitted at commit and Bitcoin-confirmed within days (a fresh proof is pending until a block lands; see VERIFY.md for per-date confirmed/pending status); prices: ERCOT public data (NP4-190, DAM SPP). Absolute USD is an upper bound (exchange fees only — no grid charges, taxes, or aggregator margin); relative metrics are robust. Verify everything yourself: see VERIFY.md in this repository.
