Paper money — no capital at stake. Every receipt is committed and pushed before the D+1 auction publishes (~13:15 CET); the ledger is append-only and OpenTimestamps-stamped — each day's proof is submitted at commit and Bitcoin-confirmed within days (a fresh proof is pending until a block lands; see VERIFY.md for per-date confirmed/pending status); prices: apidatos.ree.es, cross-checked weekly against the independent token ESIOS route.
