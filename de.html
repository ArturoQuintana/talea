The oracle is the best possible 2×2 hour choice with hindsight, same battery, same costs. Capture = P&L ÷ oracle P&L; tau = Kendall tau-b of the committed forecast vs the actual day.

Paper money — no capital at stake. Every receipt is committed and pushed before the D+1 auction publishes (EPEX SPOT DE-LU via SMARD, ~12:40-13:00 CET); the ledger is append-only and OpenTimestamps-stamped — each day's proof is submitted at commit and Bitcoin-confirmed within days (a fresh proof is pending until a block lands; see VERIFY.md for per-date confirmed/pending status); prices: Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de (CC BY 4.0). Absolute EUR is an upper bound (exchange fees only — no grid charges, taxes, or aggregator margin); relative metrics are robust. Verify everything yourself: see VERIFY.md in this repository.
